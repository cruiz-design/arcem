What it covers

Four pieces, one benefits program.

Each piece is designed alongside the other three, not separately. A benefits plan assembled piece by piece, without seeing the whole, is why most end up with gaps nobody notices until the claim.

01

Group medical and dental plans

Network, deductible and coinsurance selection negotiated with several carriers at once, compared side by side — not the first quote that comes in.

02

Life, short- and long-term disability

The coverage that holds a family together when income stops. We check that amounts scale with real salary, not a generic table.

03

Retirement and voluntary benefits

Retirement plans and voluntary options — vision, extra dental, pet, legal — that the employee pays for if they want, at no cost to the company.

04

Compliance, ACA and employee communication

The notices and deadlines the law requires, and material that explains the plan in terms an employee understands without calling HR.
