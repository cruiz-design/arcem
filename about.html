The firm

Arcem: the citadel — the part that does not give.

The name comes from Latin. It shares a root with the fortress that has stood facing the Atlantic for five hundred years, twenty minutes from our office. It is not a comfortable metaphor — it is the standard we measure a benefits program or a commercial risk program against.

Where the name comes from

The same name as El Morro.

ARCEM comes from the Latin arcem: citadel, fortress. It shares a root with El Morro, the fortification that has stood facing the Atlantic for five hundred years. The identity doesn't illustrate that idea with a castle — it translates it into a working standard: protection, vigilance, strength and permanence, applied to an insurance program instead of a wall.
